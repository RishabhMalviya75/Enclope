How It Plays

Paint yourself into the scenery, or hunt down the people who did. Here is everything in the game.

About The Game

Camo Crew is a mobile multiplayer hide-and-seek game. At the start of a round you are either a hider or a seeker. Hiders get a paint kit and a short hide phase: open the paint view, rotate and pinch-zoom around your own character, and colour your body to match the surface you plan to press yourself against. Seekers spawn in first person and sweep the map with paintballs, looking for the one patch of wall that is slightly the wrong shade.

Rounds are short, lobbies are quick, and every match runs on a live server so a friend can drop into your private room with a code — or you can matchmake into a public one.

Paint Your Body

A real paint tool, not a preset skin. Four brush sizes, step-by-step undo, and an eyedropper that lifts the exact colour off the surface behind you.

Climb, Grab & Go Prone

Jump at a wall your head can reach and you stick to it, then climb. Lie flat to disappear under furniture and along ledges.

The Whistle

Taunt the seekers with a whistle — but it is real 3D positional sound, so it tells them exactly which direction you are in.

Voice Chat

Talk to the room from the lobby onward, with per-player mute and volume. Coordinating out loud is half the fun — and half the risk.

Emotes & Dances

Eleven emotes and six looping dances, unlockable with coins. Perfect for celebrating in front of a seeker who still cannot see you.

Characters & Skins

Pick your hider body in the lobby, resize it to squeeze into tighter gaps, and kit your seeker out from the skins shop.

Four Game Modes

  • Classic — hide, blend, survive the clock.
  • Hunt — caught hiders become ghosts and spectate the seekers' point of view.
  • Infection — every hider tagged switches sides, so the hunt snowballs.
  • Reverse Chicken Race — study each hider on a pedestal, then find them out in the map.

Five Maps

  • Farm — barns, hay and open ground. Nowhere to be lazy.
  • Haunted House — dark rooms, heavy furniture and long sightlines.
  • Minecraft Village — blocky buildings, flat colours, brutally good camouflage.
  • Minecraft Garden — greenery everywhere, if you get the shade right.
  • Backrooms — endless yellow corridors where every wall looks the same.
