FAQ

Short answers to the things people ask most.

It is out now on the Google Play Store, free to download — grab it here. The old beta APK has been retired, so you do not need to sideload anything any more.
Multiplayer hide and seek. Hiders literally paint their own character — brush, colours, eyedropper — to match the surface they are hiding against, then find a spot and hold still. Seekers play in first person and hunt them with paintballs.
Yes. Create a private room and share the code, or matchmake into a public game. There is in-game voice chat from the lobby onward, with per-player mute and volume.
Five maps — Farm, Haunted House, Minecraft Village, Minecraft Garden and the Backrooms — and four modes: Classic, Hunt (caught hiders spectate the seekers), Infection (the tagged switch sides) and Reverse Chicken Race (memorise each hider before the round starts).
Open Settings in-game and drop the graphics quality to Low or Medium — the tiers exist specifically for budget devices. If it is still rough, send us your phone model on the bug report page; that is exactly the information that gets it fixed.
The Discord server is the fastest way to reach the developer, and @_enclop on Instagram gets the updates and previews.
