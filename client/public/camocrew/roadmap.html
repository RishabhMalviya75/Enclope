Roadmap

Where the game has got to, what is being built right now, and what comes next.

DONE • SHIPPED

Beta Testing

Months of direct feedback from testers: netcode stabilised, touch controls reworked, frame rate tuned across cheap and expensive Android phones.

  • Multiplayer rooms, matchmaking and host migration
  • Graphics quality tiers for low-end devices
  • Bug reports folded back into the build
DONE • LIVE NOW

Google Play Launch

Camo Crew is released and available worldwide on the Play Store, free to download, with all five maps and all four game modes in the box.

  • Public store launch complete
  • Automatic updates for everyone
  • Beta APK retired
IN PROGRESS

More Maps & Content

New places to hide, more ways to dress up, and steady polish on the modes that are already in.

  • Additional maps in the works
  • More emotes, dances and seeker skins
  • Ongoing performance work on older phones
PLANNED

Seasons & Ranked

Longer-term plans: something to climb, something to chase, and events that bring the community back each season.

  • Ranked competitive queues
  • Seasonal events and rewards
  • Wider character customisation
