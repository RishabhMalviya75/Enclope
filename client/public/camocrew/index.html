Two Roles, One Round

Hiders get a paint kit. Open it up, spin your character around, and colour yourself to match whatever you are standing next to — brick, hay, wood, the void of the Backrooms. Pick your brush size, undo a bad stroke, and use the eyedropper to lift the exact colour off the wall behind you. Then freeze, climb somewhere nobody looks, and stay quiet.

Seekers go first-person and sweep the map with paintballs. Every hider you tag is one fewer target — but a hider who nails their camouflage is genuinely invisible, and the clock is against you.

Classic

Straight hide and seek. Blend in, stay still, survive until the timer runs out.

Hunt

Get caught and you are out — but you stay in the round as a ghost, watching the seekers' own point of view.

Infection

Every hider who is tagged joins the other side. The hunt grows until nobody is left hiding.

Reverse Chicken Race

Each hider is shown on a pedestal first. Memorise every paint job — then find them again out in the map.
