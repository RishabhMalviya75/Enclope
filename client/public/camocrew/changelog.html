Changelog

What changed, and when.

v1.0 — Google Play Release LATEST
August 2026
  • Released on Google Play: free to download, with automatic updates. The beta APK is retired.
  • All four modes in: Classic, Hunt, Infection and Reverse Chicken Race.
  • Five maps: Farm, Haunted House, Minecraft Village, Minecraft Garden and the Backrooms.
  • Graphics quality tiers: Low / Medium / High so budget phones hold a steady frame rate.
Late Beta STABLE
July – August 2026
  • Emotes & dances: eleven emotes and six looping dances across every hider body.
  • Seeker skins shop: unlockable looks for the hunting side.
  • Voice chat: live room audio with per-player mute and volume.
  • Host migration: if the host leaves mid-match, the room survives.
  • Match report: a ranked end-of-round leaderboard before you head back to the lobby.
Closed Beta ARCHIVE
June – July 2026
  • Multiplayer: private rooms with join codes, global matchmaking and a shared lobby.
  • Paint tools: brush sizes, step undo and the eyedropper.
  • Movement: grab-and-climb, prone, and the whistle taunt.
Alpha Prototype ARCHIVE
Early 2026
  • Core loop: first working version of painting directly onto your own character.
  • First map: the Farm arena, stress-tested internally.
